date:   2026-03-04 09:00:00 -0500
categories: math
---

Every real polynomial function of degree $n$ can be expresed
as the sum of $n+1$ periodic functions.
The [proof](/pdf/polynomials-as-sums-of-periodic-functions.pdf)
requires the axiom of choice.
